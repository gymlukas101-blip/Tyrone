# Sound effects audit (baseline "A")

Branch: `work/monkey-sfx-foley-redesign`, based on `feature/sound-off-2` (which is based on `main` 0595444).

How it was measured, all offline and with no paid audio calls:

| What | Tool | How |
|---|---|---|
| Per-take numbers | `python -m tools.sfx.inventory` | Decodes each take with ffmpeg, so it's what the app plays.<br>Measures duration, sample peak, true peak (4× oversampled), integrated loudness (takes ≥ 0.4 s only), the loudest 10 ms RMS ("Loud 10 ms": what a short sound hits at), tail, size and SHA-256. |
| Where each cue is played | same tool | A search of `ui/src`: lines that name the cue *and* involve the sound engine. |
| Stacked-sound peaks | `python -m tools.sfx.mixcheck` | Renders the busiest moments in Chromium's own audio engine, offline. |
| Engine behavior | `ui/test/sfx.test.ts` | Vitest with a fake audio graph. |

## 1. Inventory

- 45 cues, 145 takes, 0.92 MB of Ogg, 111 s of audio, all mono.
- They decode to about **21 MB** of memory at 48 kHz, and the app decoded every take at startup.
- The full per-take table is regenerated by `python -m tools.sfx.inventory --md OUT.md`. The summary:

| Finding | Evidence | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **22 of 45 cues are never played** | No engine call names them: `check`, `chomp`, `clap`, `dizzy_loop`, `dust`, `footstep`, `jungle_loop`, `notify`, `peel`, `reward`, `sad_trombone`, `shockwave`, `slam`, `slide_down`, `steam`, `tear`, `tick`, `ticktock_loop`, `ui_close`, `ui_error`, `ui_open`, `xylo_run` | The interface had one sound, `ui_click` on every button. Confirm, error, open/close and "needs you" moments were silent. 0.4 MB is shipped unused. |
| **Takes within a cue don't match** | 18 of 42 multi-take cues spread more than 4 dB in loudest-10 ms level (`ear_perk` 22.7, `question` 12.5, `pop` 8.5, `check` 7.2, `ui_click` 6.5, `chest_thump` 6.2 dB …) | The engine's ±2 dB "variation" is swamped. Some clicks land twice as loud as others, and one `ear_perk` take is almost silent. |
| **Hot peaks** | Most takes peak at −2 to −0.2 dBTP (`pop` and `thump` −0.2) | Little headroom when beats stack (see §3). |
| **Long UI tails** | `check` tails 0.32–0.45 s, `ui_click` up to 0.35 s, `notify` 0.5–0.6 s | Fine alone. With the choke group (§4), a new UI sound now cuts the old one cleanly. |
| **The "loops" weren't loops** | `jungle_loop` (20 s), `dizzy_loop` (3 s) and `ticktock_loop` (2 s) are in the kit, but the engine had no loop playback and nothing played them | No phantom ambience today. But there was also no safe way to use them. |
| **Provenance** | The whole kit is ElevenLabs SFX. Prompts and loudness targets are in `tools/sfx/cues.json`; raw MP3s are in `tools/sfx/raw/` (115 files) | The baseline is kept untouched as candidate "A". |

## 2. Engine findings (`ui/src/avatar/sfx.ts`)

Each finding has a test in `ui/test/sfx.test.ts`, which fails on the old behavior where noted.

| # | Finding | Repro / evidence | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | **Biased shuffle bag.** `sort(() => Math.random() - 0.5)` doesn't shuffle uniformly; items tend to stay near where they started. | In Node/V8, 40 000 old-style shuffles of 4 takes led with take 1 35.8%, take 2 13.9%, take 3 19.1% and take 4 31.2% of the time (fair is 25% each). Test "Fisher-Yates is unbiased" requires 25 ± 1% | `shuffle()` (Fisher–Yates) |
| E2 | **Take levels unmatched** (see §1) | Test "takes are leveled…": three takes 8.8 dB apart now play within 0.01 dB | On decode, each take is trimmed toward its cue's median loudest-10 ms level (clamped ±6 dB). The assets aren't touched. |
| E3 | **No master protection.** Up to 8 voices at near-full-scale peaks sum straight into the output. | §3 | Soft safety limiter on the master: −6 dB threshold, 20:1, 2 ms attack, 120 ms release |
| E4 | **Click stacking.** The click from the button that opens Settings and the dialog's own sound would both ring. And every Sound Lab take button also clicked, over the take being auditioned. | Tests "a new UI sound cuts the ringing click short" and "Sound Lab preview…" | UI choke group with ranks (click < open/close < confirm < error < notify). Sound Lab buttons use `data-sfx="none"`. A new preview stops the previous one. |
| E5 | **The polyphony cap could swallow interface feedback** when 8 character sounds were ringing | Test "caps … UI still answers" | UI sounds bypass the cap. They're choked to one at a time anyway. |
| E6 | **Eager decode of everything at startup**, including 4.8 MB of loop audio nobody plays | inventory | Hot cues (click, pop, check, open/close, error, notify) are ready before `attach()` returns. The rest load in batches of 6 afterwards; loops load only when first started. |
| E7 | **No loop playback or stop path** | Tests "loop() is a real loop…" and "stopAll stops loops too" | `loop(cue)` returns a handle: real `loop = true`, deduplicated per cue, fades in, `stop()` fades out and frees it. `stopAll()` covers loops and one-shots. Nothing loops by default. |
| E8 | **Sound could ring on after the window is hidden** (to the tray) | code path | `visibilitychange` (hidden) calls `stopAll()` |
| E9 | First click silent? | Test "hot cues are ready before attach() returns" | Covered by E6 |

Checked and **not** a bug:
- The polyphony bookkeeping releases voices when they end (test "voices are released").
- The cooldown already stops rapid-click spam: 30 clicks in 300 ms give ≤ 8 sounds, only one ringing.
- Ducking under speech and listening was already in place: −10 dB, 50 ms attack, 300 ms release.
- A missing file fails quietly, and the other takes still play (test).

## 3. Stacked peaks (real Chromium audio engine)

Worst case, from `tools/sfx/mixcheck.py`:
- the loudest take of each cue;
- every take at +2 dB jitter;
- volume at 100%;
- the beat timings from `ui/src/avatar/events.ts`.

| Moment | Without limiter: peak | Clipped samples | With limiter: peak | Clipped |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| mini chest-beat | +1.0 dBFS | 38 | -3.7 dBFS | 0 |
| wake startle | +2.6 dBFS | 218 | -3.9 dBFS | 0 |
| sit down | +1.7 dBFS | 132 | -4.1 dBFS | 0 |
| celebrate | +1.3 dBFS | 74 | -3.7 dBFS | 0 |
| click → open → error | -3.6 dBFS | 0 | -5.2 dBFS | 0 |

4 of 5 busy moments clipped at full volume before the limiter.
- With it, nothing clips, and the peaks land between about −3.7 and −5.2 dBFS.
- The Web Audio compressor adds automatic makeup gain: **+2.91 dB** below its threshold, measured in Chromium. The engine takes that back out with a fixed gain after the limiter, so a single sound plays at exactly its old level.
- Before that fix, the "limiter" made everything 2.9 dB louder. The first measurement caught it.

## 4. What changed in the app (this branch)

| Moment | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Any button | `ui_click` | `ui_click`, except buttons that make their own sound (`data-sfx`) |
| Settings, Memory, Tasks, the Brain table, any dialog opens / closes | click only | `ui_open` / `ui_close`, replacing the click |
| A ✓ result toast ("✓ Claude key works") | silent | `check` |
| A ⚠ problem toast, warning or error | silent | `ui_error` (warning softer) |
| A draft waiting for your OK; a computer task asking permission; an update ready to install | silent | `notify`, the one "look here" chime, with a 2 s cooldown |
| Sound Lab take buttons | click over the take | the take alone; a new take stops the previous one |
| Window hidden to the tray | sounds could ring on | everything stops |

UI levels follow the measured loudness, so the interface has a hierarchy, all under speech:

| Cue | Short-term level |
|---|---|
| click | −15 |
| open / close | −15 |
| confirm | −14 |
| error | −13 |
| notification | −11 |

## 5. Not done yet / needs the owner

- **Blind listening of the new candidates.**
  - No shipped take was replaced. 13 cues have two CC0 candidate sets each (B, C; `docs/audio/SOURCE_LEDGER.md`), rendered by `tools/sfx/recipe.py`.
  - They're audible two ways:
    - the blind review page: 12 cards, the current set vs B vs C, loudness-matched, randomized per listener (built by `tools/sfx/abclips.py`);
    - the in-app Sound Lab: a **Now · B · C** switch per cue, which applies everywhere at once and is saved in settings.
  - A candidate replaces the shipped set only if listeners clearly prefer it. Otherwise the current sound stays.
  - **AI listening scores: not evaluated.** No reviewer here can hear raw audio, and scores from spectrograms would not be listening.
- **The near-silent `ear_perk` take.** Leveling lifts it by at most 6 dB, so it stays quiet. Turning it off is a Sound Lab click (right-click the take); that's the owner's call, so I left it on.
- **Measurements on the Windows PC:**
  - trigger-to-sound latency (WebView2's `outputLatency`);
  - how it sounds on laptop speakers.

  This container has no audio device.
- **Ambience** (`jungle_loop`) stays off. Turning on background audio for everyone needs approval.
