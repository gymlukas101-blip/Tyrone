# Monkey mascot: reference study (generic principles only)

*Researched 2026-11-18. The references inform proportions, shape language and appeal only. Their names, images and signature elements never go into image prompts. Head-to-body ratios are rough estimates from the official galleries.*

## References studied
| Reference | What we learn (generically) |
|---|---|
| Donkey Kong (2023 movie / Bananza 2025) | The redesign aimed for "more expressive": softer, calmer brows, bigger eyes, a wide mouth. About 1:2.5 head-to-body, huge chest and forearms, short bowed legs, stylized fur that reads as shapes, warm browns with one saturated accent [1–7] |
| Winston (Overwatch) | Silhouette first: a gargoyle-like gorilla mass. The comedy and heart come from contrast, a gentle intellectual in a brute body. Accessories carry story and the tech is built in, not stuck on [8–13] |
| Pat Fusty (Bloons TD 6) | Big, chubby, brown fur with a tan face. Comedy from sheer size. A strong base silhouette survives big costume changes (skins) [14–15] |
| King Louie, Mojo Jojo, Ape Escape monkeys, Diddy Kong | Personality through pose and swagger. Pointy ears and sharp angles read as villainous. Accessories can work as status UI (a helmet light shows alert level). A small, nimble sidekick contrasts with a big bruiser [16–23] |

## Principles we borrow
1. **Mass up top, small at the bottom.** A barrel chest and forearms over short legs reads as strong, harmless and funny.
2. **Rounded masses on a square-ish structure.** Triangles only as small accents, like the hair tuft.
3. **Soft resting brows.** Strength comes from the body, not a scowl.
4. **Big eyes and a wide, flexible mouth.** Essential for a lip-syncing voice assistant.
5. **Silhouette first.** It must read as a solid black shape at icon size.
6. **Contrast is the comedy:** a gentle, smart mind in a big body.
7. **Accessories do a job.** The banana headset with its cyan glow shows listening, thinking and speaking.
8. **One saturated accent on a warm, earthy base.** For us: cyan glow plus banana yellow.
9. **Fur reads as clumped shapes, not strands.**
10. **A light muzzle and face mask** frame expressions and read at small sizes.
11. **A stable core identity**, so outfits can change later.
12. **Pose and voice carry the personality.**
13. **No villain cues:** pointy ears, sharp angles.

## Knockoff pitfalls (never use these)
- A red necktie with initials.
- A DK-style curled hair tuft. Ours is spiky.
- A red ball cap with a logo badge.
- Round glasses combined with armor plates, a scientist identity, or a cannon.
- A zig-zag head pattern, or a black headband combined with yellow hand wraps.
- A dome helmet with a cape.
- A helmet with a siren light, or color-coded shorts.
- An orangutan king persona with a crown.

General test: never pair two signature elements from one character, and check the silhouette against every reference.
